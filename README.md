## Featured Projects

### 1. Claims Management Platform Business Case

Developed a structured business case for a proposed health-insurance claims management platform. The work included requirements analysis, stakeholder identification, options appraisal, SWOT analysis, cost-benefit analysis, a RACI matrix and a communication plan.

**Role:** Business Change Analyst
**Methods:** Business case, SWOT, cost-benefit analysis, stakeholder analysis and RACI

### 2. Insurance Policy Sales and Revenue Performance Dashboard

Designed a five-page Power BI report providing insights into policy revenue, premium trends, product performance, marketing campaigns and geographic performance.

**Role:** BI Developer
**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query and data modelling

### 3. Healthcare Patient Admissions Dashboard

Created an operational Power BI dashboard for analysing patient admissions, diagnosis patterns, treatment outcomes and admission trends. The report included patient-level drill-through functionality.

**Role:** BI Developer
**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query and data visualisation

### 4. E-Cigarette Market and Regulatory Dashboard

Developed a four-page Power BI report combining sales performance with FDA PMTA authorisation data to support commercial and regulatory risk monitoring.

**Role:** BI Developer
**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query and regulatory analytics

### 5. Retail and Grocery Operations Dashboard

Designed a Power BI report analysing retail revenue, department performance, customer behaviour, hourly ordering patterns and product profitability.

**Role:** BI Developer
**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query and DAX

### 6. Global Sporting Goods Sales Performance Analysis

Analysed 113,058 sales transactions covering six years, six countries and three product categories. The analysis identified revenue trends, profit drivers, leading markets and high-performing products.

**Key results:** £95.0 million in revenue, £42.1 million in profit and a 44.3% profit margin
**Tools:** SQL Server, Excel, Power Query and PivotTables

### 7. UK Gym Membership Retention Analysis

Analysed 250 gym memberships across five UK regions to investigate retention, withdrawal, engagement and membership-fee patterns.

**Key insight:** Withdrawn members recorded the highest average visit frequency, suggesting that churn was not simply caused by declining engagement.
**Tools:** Excel, Power Query and PivotTables

### 8. Banking Cloud Migration Gap Analysis

Conducted a structured gap analysis for a banking cloud-migration case study. Applied SWOT, Fishbone, PESTLE and McKinsey 7S frameworks to diagnose current-state challenges and develop a prioritised three-phase, 24-month roadmap.

**Role:** Business Analyst
**Methods:** Gap analysis, stakeholder workshops, SWOT, Fishbone, PESTLE and McKinsey 7S

### 9. Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Used PostgreSQL to analyse 3,547 point-of-sale transactions covering 381 days. The analysis identified best-selling products, revenue drivers and sales trends.

**Key result:** Latte and Americano with Milk generated 46% of total revenue.
**Tools:** PostgreSQL and pgAdmin

### 10. Retail Orders and Product SQL Analysis

Used PostgreSQL to answer retail and merchandising questions involving departmental order volumes, unsold products and product catalogues.

**Techniques:** Joins, aggregation, `LEFT JOIN`, `IS NULL` and grouped queries
**Tools:** PostgreSQL
